ToneFFX ↗

Audio / Software

A guitar tone comparison tool. Match your recording against a reference track and get specific EQ and brightness adjustments.

Behind the build +

I built the audio analysis, recording interface, and saved equipment settings. Chroma-based dynamic time warping aligns different performances before comparing seven frequency bands.

Python · librosa · Next.js · Supabase

02

Allos ↗

Computer vision

A computer vision prototype that traces potential allergen contact through hands, utensils, and meals.

Behind the build +

Built at Hack the Valley. Overhead camera observations become a history of contacts, so a possible exposure can be traced to its source. Hands and utensil surfaces maintain independent state.

Computer vision · Contact tracking · Event history

A team prototype that estimates potential contact; it does not detect allergens or certify food safety.

03

Giraffe ↗

Embedded / Wearables

A wearable posture monitor built with a motion sensor, an Arduino, and a buzzer. Theme Prize at vsHacks 2025.

Behind the build +

I developed the sensor firmware and helped assemble the wearable with two teammates. We combined accelerometer and gyroscope readings, configured the MPU-6050 at the register level, and fitted the electronics into a 3D-printed enclosure.

C++ · Arduino Nano · MPU-6050

Theme Prize, vsHacks 2025.

04

Solar-powered boat

Hardware

A boat prototype that stores solar energy in capacitors before powering its motor. Built for UBC Physics Olympics.

Behind the build +

I led four teammates through prototyping for UBC Physics Olympics. My work covered the energy-storage and motor-control circuitry, STM32 selection for low-power operation, and a 60-second activation delay.

Circuit design · STM32 · Prototyping
